Now that it’s the Christmas holidays it is of course time to write about the Summer.

## sLOVEnia
Apologies for the corny title; I did really enjoy it though :)

This summer I was fortunate enough to visit Slovenia—staying in Lake Bled, but also visiting Lake Bohinj, capital city Ljubljana, Radovljica, Kojsko, Dobrovo, and various other places. Naturally I took the Nikon with me and tried to be as creative as possible. This was a holiday first and foremost—not a photography focused trip—so I was more concerned with having a relaxing time rather than getting up at 4am to capture a mega sunrise.  Nevertheless, I think I managed to get some lovely shots; the Nikon makes this reasonably easy!

A select gallery of images can be found [here](https://www.jonprobert.co.uk/slovenia-2024). Or an extended 75 image gallery [here on adobe portfolio](https://jonprobert.myportfolio.com/slovenia).

## Jeju IWEF

After a solid 29 hours of travelling from the UK, I had the amazing opportunity to attend the Jeju IWEF (International Wind Ensemble Festival) with Foden’s Band ([band photos here](https://www.jonprobert.co.uk/south-korea-fodens-band)). As well as the festival, the island is famed for the quality of its sumo-mandarins (called Hallabong), local black-pig pork, and fresh seafood caught by Jeju's famous female deep-sea divers (haenyeo).

Image

Although the festival was the primary reason for the trip, I 100% had to bring the camera with me. Lack of luggage space meant I had to adapt my instrument case to accommodate the camera. I was therefore limited to the Nikon Z7ii with a singular all purpose lens, the 24-70mm f/2.8.

Travelling with a group is not always conducive to ‘good’ photography—often not giving us enough time to properly compose a shot, or wait for the right light. But you make the best of it, and I think I’ve come away with some cool shots here regardless.
